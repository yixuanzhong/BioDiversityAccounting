SG Biodiversity Accounting Metric

Interactive edition of Metric 1.1 and its condition criteria

Metric calculator

Enter the habitat parcels on your site before development (baseline), then what will be created and enhanced. The metric converts each parcel into biodiversity units = area × distinctiveness × condition and compares the two snapshots. Terrestrial, freshwater and marine units are kept separate and are never traded between ecosystems.

Project

Nothing you type is saved or sent anywhere. Your inputs live only in this browser tab. A share link only carries them if you choose to include them.

Terrestrial habitat condition assessment

Score each criterion from your survey data (vegetation plots, fauna transects, walkover). The total sets the parcel's condition: Good (3), Moderate (2) or Poor (1), which is the condition multiplier in the metric. Criteria follow the Terrestrial Habitat Condition Criteria 1.1 workbook.

Freshwater habitat condition assessment

Score each criterion for the freshwater parcel. Criteria follow the Freshwater Habitat Condition Criteria 1.1 workbook. Marine condition criteria are still in development by the metric authors, so marine conditions are entered directly in the calculator.

Habitat guide

All 42 habitat types in the Singapore habitat classification, with the scores behind their distinctiveness, how hard they are to create or enhance, how long they take to reach target condition, and what compensation is required if they are lost.

Calculations, habitat data and condition criteria come from the SG Biodiversity Accounting Metric 1.1. Cite as: AECOM & Camphora (2024). Singapore Biodiversity Accounting Metric: Version 1.1. Available at anz.planengage.com/singapore-bio-metric. This independent interactive edition does not replace the official tool or expert ecological advice.
